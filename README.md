## 📱 iPhone Setup (Add to Home Screen)

1. Open the site in **Safari**
2. Tap the **Share** button (bottom center)
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **Add**

The app will appear on your home screen and open fullscreen like a native app. All your progress is saved locally on the device.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Daily timeline** with study sessions, workouts, free blocks
- **Checkboxes** — tap to mark tasks complete
- **Progress tracking** — overall %, streak, study hours, letter score
- **Per-subject analytics** — MECH306 / MECH302 / MECH304 progress bars
- **Activity heatmap** — visual overview of study days
- **Add / Edit / Delete tasks** — fully editable schedule
- **Drag & drop** — move tasks between days
- **Export / Import** — backup your data as JSON
- **Offline** — works without internet after first load
- **Local storage** — progress persists between sessions
